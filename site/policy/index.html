Ship Ahead: Volcanic (Math)

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

In short: Ship Ahead does not ask for an account and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Player names you type into the game stay on your device. There are no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases in the current version.

Who we are

Ship Ahead: Volcanic (Math) ("Ship Ahead", "the app") is a quiz board game made by Ashmaz Technologies, a business registered in Malaysia ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the Ship Ahead app on Google Play (package com.ashmaz.shipahead.volcanicmath) and the game it loads.

Questions about this policy: shipahead-support@ashmaz.app

Information the app keeps on your device

To set up a game, you can type a name for each player and choose subjects, difficulty, a timer and player colours. The app saves these settings, and whether you have watched the opening story, on your device so they are ready next time. This information:

  • stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else;
  • can be any nickname, and does not need to be a real name;
  • is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Information sent when the game loads

The app loads the game over a secure (HTTPS) connection from the web server that hosts it. Like any website, that server receives basic technical information needed to deliver the game: your IP address, the type of device and browser, and the time of the request. Our hosting provider may keep this in short-term server logs for security and to keep the service running. We do not use it to identify you, build a profile of you, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in or email address.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No advertising ID and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No payment information.

The only Android permission the app uses is internet access, which it needs to load the game.

Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no advertisements and has no in-app purchases. If we add them later:

  • Ads will come only from an ad network that Google Play certifies for apps used by children, set to show non-personalised, child-appropriate ads. The ads will not use information about you or your child to target them.
  • Any purchase, such as removing ads, will be handled by Google Play. We will not receive your card or payment details.
  • We will update this policy, and its effective date, before the version with ads or purchases is released.

Children

Ship Ahead is made for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Player names are kept on the device only, so we never receive them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. The only third party involved is the company that hosts the game, which processes the technical information described above on our behalf. We may disclose information if the law requires us to.

Keeping information safe

The game is delivered only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and the app refuses to open pages from any other website. Settings on your device are protected by your device's own security.

Your choices and rights

You can delete everything the app stores at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Because we do not collect personal information, we will usually have nothing to return, but we will always answer.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
