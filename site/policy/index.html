Ship Ahead: Volcanic (Math)

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

In short: Ship Ahead has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Games on one device stay on that device. If you choose Play online, the nickname and colour you pick and your moves in that game are sent to our game server so the other players in your room can see them. Online rooms are deleted about 6 hours after the last move.

Who we are

Ship Ahead: Volcanic (Math) ("Ship Ahead", "the app") is a quiz board game made by Ashmaz Technologies, a business registered in Malaysia ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the Ship Ahead app on Google Play (package com.ashmaz.shipahead.volcanicmath) and to the web version of the game.

Questions about this policy: shipahead-support@ashmaz.app

Playing on one device

The game, its questions, pictures and music are built into the app. When you play alone or pass the phone between players, everything happens on your device and nothing is sent to us. The app saves these on your device so they are ready next time:

  • player names and avatar colours you type in setup (any nickname is fine);
  • game settings such as school level, question types, difficulty, timers, weather and sound;
  • whether you have watched the opening story;
  • a room pass for any online game you joined, so you can rejoin your seat.

This information is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Playing online

Play online is optional. When you create or join an online room, the app sends these to our game server:

  • the nickname (up to 18 characters) and avatar colour you choose;
  • the game settings chosen by the player who creates the room;
  • your moves during the game: dice rolls, answers, powers you use and the player you target;
  • a random room pass that identifies your seat in that room.

The server uses this only to run the game: it rolls the dice, checks answers, keeps the score and shows every player the same board. Everyone in the same room can see each player's nickname, colour and score, whether each answer was right, and the final standings. There is no public list of rooms, no public leaderboard, and no chat.

The room's code can be shared as an invite link. If you share it, you choose who receives it.

How long we keep it: an online room, with its nicknames and game data, expires 6 hours after the last change to the game and is then deleted from our database. Deletion can take a little longer than 6 hours, because expired rooms are cleared when the server next tidies up.

Technical information: like any internet service, the servers that host the game receive the basic information needed to answer a request, such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the time. The game does not store this in its database or use it to identify or track you. Our hosting provider may keep it in short-term server logs for security and reliability.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No advertising ID, analytics or tracking tools.
  • No payment information.

The only Android permission the app uses is internet access, for online play and for opening links such as this policy.

Advertising and purchases

The current version shows no advertisements and has no in-app purchases. If we add them later:

  • Ads will come only from an ad network that Google Play certifies for apps used by children, set to show non-personalised, child-appropriate ads.
  • Any purchase, such as removing ads, will be handled by Google Play. We will not receive your card or payment details.
  • We will update this policy, and its effective date, before the version with ads or purchases is released.

Children

Ship Ahead is made for players of all ages, including children. We do not ask for anyone's real name, age, email or other contact details. In online play, other players in the same room see only the nickname and colour a player chooses, so we suggest children use a nickname rather than their real name. Parents and guardians can turn off online play simply by playing on one device. If you believe your child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Online game data is processed by the company that hosts our game server and database, only to run the game on our behalf. We may disclose information if the law requires us to.

Keeping information safe

Online play uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Each seat in an online room is protected by a random room pass that only that player's device holds. Information saved on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Your choices and rights

  • Play on one device if you do not want anything sent online.
  • Clear the app's data or uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your device.
  • Online room data deletes itself about 6 hours after the last move. To ask for it to be deleted sooner, email us with the room code.

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
