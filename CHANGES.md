# Changes since board-prototype

Compared with board-prototype at 540d967, the live game includes:

- A skippable animated pixel-art beacon-repair story, with harbour, beacon and trail scenes.
- A math-focused setup with four school bands, mixed selections and random selection. The original question timer and question formats remain.
- A whole-match eruption clock, time-expiry scoring and escape standings.
- Animated island life, storm conditions, player danger reactions, closer camera tracking and revised dice and movement sequences.
- Six illustrated crew members, volcano and rescue-ferry art, a recorded soundtrack, synthesized effects and separate volume controls.
- Online rooms with invitations, server-side turns, timed questions, reconnect tokens and presence checks.

The source export also vendors the fonts and their licences, builds file-compatible scripts, makes story paths portable, and provides an explicit online-lobby entry when opened from a file. The worker is a separate build target. The original Three.js demo is included.
